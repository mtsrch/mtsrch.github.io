Offline magnet search for Android

Magnet Search is a free Android app for searching a magnet index without sending your queries to a search service. The index is bundled inside the APK, so search works without a network connection, including in airplane mode. The app requires no account and contains no advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting SDK.

Magnet Search 是一款 Android 离线磁力搜索工具。磁力索引内置在安装包中，搜索词只在本机数据库查询， 不会上传到服务器；无需注册账号，也不包含广告、统计或崩溃上报 SDK。

Features

  • Search the bundled magnet index by name, tag, file extension, or sort order.
  • View file lists, first-seen and last-seen timestamps, and copy magnet links or infohashes.
  • Save favorites and recent searches locally on your device.
  • Chinese and English interface, with no account and no cloud sync.
  • Small download, Android 8.0 and later, package io.github.mtsrch.

How to install

Download the signed APK above and open it on your Android device. Android may ask you to allow installs from your browser or file manager; enable that permission for the installer, complete the install, then turn the permission off again if you do not need it. The app does not need Google Play Services to run.

Frequently asked questions

Does Magnet Search work offline?

Yes. The magnet index is included in the APK and searches run only in the local database. No search request is sent to a server.

Does it collect personal data?

No account or identity information is requested. Favorites, search history, and language preference stay in the app’s private storage on your device. See the privacy policy.

Is the APK signed?

Yes. Every GitHub release APK is signed, and a SHA-256 checksum is published next to the download.
