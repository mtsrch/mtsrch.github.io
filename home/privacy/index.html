Privacy Policy

Magnet Search · io.github.mtsrch · Effective 2026-11-26 · Applies to version 1.1.5 and later

This policy explains how the Magnet Search Android app handles your information. Magnet Search is provided by developer bzl and is intended for use as is. In short: favorites and search history stay on your device; search uses a bundled offline index; the app contains no advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting SDK.

By using the app, you agree to the information practices described here. Terms used in this policy have the same meaning as in applicable law and the Google Play Terms of Service.

1. Information we collect and store

Magnet Search does not require an account and does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, or other identity information. The app stores only the following preferences and actions on your device:

FavoritesInfohashes you starred, used to show them in “Favorites”.
Search historyUp to 20 recent search terms, de-duplicated and ordered newest first, used to refill searches.
LanguageYour selected interface language (Chinese or English).

This data is stored only in the app’s private Android storage (SharedPreferences). It is not uploaded to our servers and is not shared with advertisers, analytics providers, or other third parties. The app does not actively read the Android Advertising ID or collect a device identifier.

2. How search works

Magnet search runs against an offline index bundled with the app. Your search terms are used only for local database queries and are not sent to a search service, server, or third party. Search therefore works without a network connection, including in airplane mode.

3. Log and diagnostic data

The app does not collect, write, or upload logs that identify you, and does not upload favorites, search history, or search terms. The magnet index is bundled with the APK, and searches run only in the local database.

The magnet edition does not include poster browsing or remote image loading and makes no network requests in normal use. If you tap the online privacy policy link, the app hands the URL to your system browser; the browser, not the app, makes the network request.

The app contains no crash-reporting SDK. Crash, diagnostic, or installation data collected by Google Play or Android is platform-side data handled under Google’s privacy policy, not data collected inside the app.

4. Cookies and local storage

This is a native Android app. It does not use cookies as an account identifier or for cross-app tracking. Favorites, search history, and language preference are stored locally using Android SharedPreferences.

If you open an external website or app store from the app, that third party may use cookies or similar technologies and handles data under its own privacy policy. This policy does not cover third-party websites.

5. Permissions

The app declares two standard Android permissions. Neither is required for its current features:

  • INTERNET — Declared in the manifest but not used for search or data collection. Magnet search runs against the bundled offline index. Opening the online privacy policy passes the link to the system browser.
  • ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE — Declared in the manifest but not read by the app.

The app does not request location, contacts, SMS, camera, microphone, photos/files, storage, or device identifier permissions, and does not read data belonging to other apps.

6. Service providers and sharing

The app contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting, push-notification, or social-login SDK, and does not actively read the Android Advertising ID. We do not sell, rent, or trade user data.

Google Play, as the app distribution platform, may process installation, purchase, and crash data under Google’s privacy policy. The app itself does not include Google Play Services advertising or analytics components.

The magnet edition does not contact an image or asset host. The only third-party service involved in distribution is Google Play.

Information may be disclosed only when required to comply with applicable law or valid legal process; to protect the safety and rights of users, the developer, or the public; or to investigate a serious service security issue.

7. Retention and deletion

Favorites, search history, and language preference remain on your device until you act:

  • remove a favorite in “Settings → Favorites”;
  • clear history in “Settings → Search history”;
  • clear app data in Android system settings;
  • uninstall the app.

Favorites, search history, and language preference have no developer-server copy. Clearing app data or uninstalling removes the local data.

8. Links to other sites

The app may contain links to external websites or app stores. If you click a third-party link, you will be directed to a site we do not operate. We have no control over its content or privacy practices, and we recommend reviewing its privacy policy before use.

9. Children’s privacy

This app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the app requires no account and collects no name, email address, or location, it does not normally hold children’s personal data. Contact us if you believe a child’s information has been handled improperly.

10. Security

Local data is stored in the app’s private Android directory and protected by the operating system sandbox. Favorites and search history remain on the device and are not uploaded. The app does not send favorites, search history, or search terms to a server.

11. Changes

If app features or data practices change, we will update this page and revise the effective date. Material changes will be noted in the app or on the store listing. Changes are effective when posted.
